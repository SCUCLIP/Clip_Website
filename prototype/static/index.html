What CLIP does

CLIP is a student-led club at Santa Clara University. We connect students with industry leaders, build skills you don't get in class, and show how coursework turns into real drug discovery, molecular engineering, biotech and AI for healthcare.

Read the full entry

  • Talks with industry leaders

    Meet people working in biotech, pharma and medical devices, on campus and off.

  • Skill-building

    Build skills for drug discovery, molecular engineering, biotech and AI in healthcare.

  • Connections across the Bay Area

    Work with Bay Area organizations and other SCU clubs on events and projects.
